## 🏢 About This Business

**Varito Solutions** is a Chattogram-based e-commerce business targeting the Bangladesh market.

### Product Lines
1. **Sanitary Items** — Full range from luxury fittings to minimum/budget fittings (bathroom, kitchen, plumbing hardware)
2. **Packaging Materials** — Everything needed for product delivery:
   - Cartons (all sizes)
   - Tape (transparent, brown, masking, OPP)
   - Bubble wrap
   - Poly bags / poly mailers
   - Stretch film / shrink wrap
   - Labels and stickers
   - Foam sheets / corner protectors
   - Any material needed for safe product packaging

### Business Models (Hybrid)
- **Reseller** — Buy from Chattogram local wholesalers, sell online with markup
- **Dropshipping** — Supplier ships directly, no stock held
- **Own Brand** — Source/manufacture under Varito brand (long-term goal)

### Base Location
**Chattogram, Bangladesh** — leveraging local wholesale network
